Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Man's Best Friend ("we," "us") collects and uses information when you visit this website or contact us about dog boarding.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you call, text, email or send a stay request, you may share your name, phone number, email, neighborhood, travel dates and details about your dog (such as breed, age, health needs and veterinarian contact).
  • Website usage. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. If we add analytics tools in the future, this policy will be updated.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule your dog's stay
  • To care for your dog, including following feeding, medical and emergency instructions
  • To contact you during a stay and about future bookings

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it when needed to care for your dog (for example, with a veterinarian in an emergency), when required by law, or with service providers who help us run this website.

The stay request form

The request form on our Contact page does not store information on this website. It opens your own email app with your details filled in, and your message is sent from your email account to ours.

Data retention & security

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our services and maintain our records, and we take reasonable steps to protect it.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by emailing geistandrew@yahoo.com or calling (305) 322-3338.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
