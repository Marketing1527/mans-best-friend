About Man's Best Friend

A small, home-based dog boarding service in Pinecrest, run by Andrew Geist.

Care that feels like family

Man's Best Friend started with a simple idea: when you leave town, your dog shouldn't be stuck alone at home or behind a kennel door. They should be somewhere that feels like home, with someone who genuinely cares.

That's what we offer. Dogs stay with Andrew in his Pinecrest home, with a small group of no more than 10 dogs, so every dog gets real attention. Most of our families have one or two dogs and use us when they travel or have an event, for anything from a few nights to about a month.

Many families find us through neighbors, local community chats and word of mouth, and we're proud of that. The best compliment is a family coming back trip after trip.
