Our service

In-Home Dog Boarding

Your dog stays with us in our Pinecrest home, with a comfortable routine, good company and a lot of love, while you travel.

What in-home boarding means for your dog

A kennel can be loud, stressful and lonely. Our home is the opposite. Your dog spends the day in a real house, sleeps somewhere comfortable, and is looked after by the same person every day.

We keep the group small so the house stays calm and every dog gets individual attention, from mealtimes and potty breaks to playtime and cuddles.

  • Overnight stays in a home, not a kennel
  • Your dog's own food and feeding schedule
  • Medications given as instructed
  • Daily routine built around your dog
  • Plenty of company and attention

At a glance

  • $50 per night, per dog
  • Small groups, so every dog gets attention
  • Stays from a few nights up to about a month
  • Based in Pinecrest
  • Book by phone or text, no online booking

Before the stay

What to bring

Keeping your dog's routine familiar helps them settle in fast.

Food

Enough of their regular food for the whole stay, plus a little extra.

Medications
